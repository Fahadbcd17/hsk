1|动词 + 好|finish doing; get ready|Verb + 好 + object|好 after a verb shows that the action is complete and its result is ready for use. Put 没 before the verb to say it is not ready.|我已经买好票了。|I have already bought the tickets.|你准备好了吗？|Are you ready?
1|一……也/都不/没……|not even one; not at all|一 + measure word + noun + 也/都 + 不/没 + verb|Use 一 with a measure word and 也 or 都 before a negative to emphasize that there are no exceptions. 一点儿也不 can negate an adjective.|我一本书也没买。|I did not buy a single book.|他一点儿也不累。|He is not tired at all.
1|那|then; in that case|Previous situation + 那 + response|那 introduces a response or suggestion based on what was just said. Here it means “in that case”, rather than the demonstrative “that”.|你没有时间？那我们明天去吧。|You have no time? Then let us go tomorrow.|今天下雨，那就不出去了。|It is raining today, so we will not go out.
2|动词 + 来/去|movement toward or away from the speaker|Verb + 来/去|来 shows movement toward the speaker and 去 movement away. A place object normally comes before 来 or 去.|请进来。|Please come in.|他回学校去了。|He has gone back to school.
2|动词一 + 了 + 就 + 动词二|do the second action immediately after the first|Verb 1 + 了 + 就 + verb 2|了 marks completion of the first action and 就 introduces what follows immediately. The first action may also be a future condition.|我吃了饭就去学校。|I will go to school as soon as I have eaten.|他回来了就给我打电话。|He calls me as soon as he gets back.
2|能……吗？|how could …? (a rhetorical question)|能 + statement + 吗？|This rhetorical question implies the opposite of the literal question. Context makes the expected answer clear.|你每天吃这么多，能不胖吗？|You eat so much every day. How could you not gain weight?|他帮了我，我能不谢谢他吗？|He helped me. How could I not thank him?
3|还是 / 或者|or in a question / or in a statement|Choice A + 还是/或者 + choice B|Use 还是 to ask someone to choose. Use 或者 to present alternatives in a statement. An embedded choice question can still use 还是.|你喝茶还是咖啡？|Would you like tea or coffee?|我想买苹果或者香蕉。|I want to buy apples or bananas.
3|地方 + 动词着 + 数量 + 名词|there is something in a place|Location + verb + 着 + number + measure word + noun|This structure describes an ongoing arrangement or state. The noun after the verb introduces the thing present at that location.|桌子上放着一本书。|There is a book lying on the desk.|门口站着两个人。|Two people are standing at the door.
3|会|may; likely to|Situation + 会 + result|会 can predict a likely result. In this use it describes possibility, not a learned skill.|晚上会下雨。|It will probably rain tonight.|喝杯热茶会很舒服。|A cup of hot tea will feel comforting.
4|又……又……|both … and …|又 + adjective 1 + 又 + adjective 2|Use 又 before each of two parallel descriptions of the same person or thing. The descriptions should fit the same overall evaluation.|这个苹果又大又甜。|This apple is both big and sweet.|她又聪明又认真。|She is both clever and conscientious.
4|动词一着……动词二……|do one action while maintaining another|Verb 1 + 着 + object + verb 2|着 marks the accompanying state or action. The second verb gives the main action happening at the same time.|他拿着书说话。|He talks while holding a book.|她笑着回答问题。|She answers questions with a smile.
5|了|a situation has changed|New situation + 了|Sentence-final 了 signals a new situation or change. A negative sentence with 不……了 can mean “no longer”.|天气不冷了。|The weather is no longer cold.|我现在会写这个字了。|I can write this character now.
5|越来越……|more and more …|越来越 + adjective / mental-state verb|越来越 describes an increasing degree over time. Do not add 很 between 越来越 and the adjective.|天气越来越热了。|The weather is getting hotter and hotter.|我越来越喜欢这里了。|I like this place more and more.
6|动词 + 得/不 + 结果|can / cannot achieve a result|Verb + 得/不 + result or direction|Use 得 to show that a result is achievable and 不 to show it is not. This differs from describing how well an action is actually done.|这个字太小，我看不清楚。|This character is too small for me to see clearly.|你听得懂吗？|Can you understand what you hear?
6|名词 + 呢？|where is …?|Noun + 呢？|A noun followed by 呢 can ask where that person or thing is. Context distinguishes this from “what about …?”.|我的手机呢？|Where is my mobile phone?|小丽呢？她还没来吗？|Where is Xiaoli? Hasn't she arrived yet?
6|刚 / 刚才|just did something / a short time ago|刚 + verb; 刚才 + clause|刚 is an adverb before the verb and stresses a recent action. 刚才 is a time word that can appear before the subject or before the verb.|我刚到家。|I have just arrived home.|刚才他给我打电话了。|He called me just now.
7|动词 + 时长|do something for a length of time|Verb + 了 + duration + object; verb + object + repeated verb + duration|A duration says how long an action lasts. With a verb-object expression, place the duration between verb and object or repeat the verb after the object.|我学了两年汉语。|I studied Chinese for two years.|他看电视看了一个小时。|He watched television for an hour.
7|对……感兴趣|be interested in …|Subject + 对 + topic + 感兴趣|对 introduces the topic of interest. Put 很、更、非常 or 不 before 感兴趣 to change the degree or negate it.|我对中国文化感兴趣。|I am interested in Chinese culture.|她对音乐更感兴趣。|She is more interested in music.
7|半 / 刻 / 差|half / quarter / minutes to an hour|Hour + 点 + 半; hour + 点 + 一刻; 差 + minutes + next hour|半 means half an hour, 一刻 a quarter of an hour, and 差 introduces the time remaining before the next hour.|我们七点半见面。|We will meet at half past seven.|现在差一刻八点。|It is a quarter to eight now.
8|又 / 再|again in a completed event / again later|又 + verb; 再 + verb|又 usually refers to repetition that has already happened. 再 usually refers to another action that will happen later or is requested.|他今天又迟到了。|He was late again today.|明天再来吧。|Come again tomorrow.
8|哪儿……就哪儿……|wherever …, there …|Question word in clause 1 + 就 + same question word in clause 2|Repeat the same question word to link two matching choices. The second choice follows the first, so this is a statement rather than a question.|你去哪儿，我就去哪儿。|I will go wherever you go.|你吃什么，我就吃什么。|I will eat whatever you eat.
9|越……越……|the more …, the more …|越 + change A + 越 + change B|The degree of the second action or state changes together with the first. The subjects of the two clauses may be different.|雨越下越大。|The rain is getting heavier and heavier.|你越认真，我越放心。|The more conscientious you are, the less I worry.
9|跟……一样|as … as; the same as …|A + 跟 + B + 一样 + adjective|Use 一样 to show equality in a stated quality. Put 不 before 一样 to say the two are different.|我跟姐姐一样高。|I am as tall as my older sister.|这本书跟那本书不一样。|This book is different from that one.
10|比……一点儿/得多/多了|a little / much more … than …|A + 比 + B + adjective + degree or difference|State the difference after the adjective. 一点儿 or 一些 gives a small difference; 得多 or 多了 gives a large one. A number can give an exact difference.|他比我高一点儿。|He is a little taller than me.|数学比历史难多了。|Maths is much harder than history.
10|两三 / 七八|two or three / seven or eight|Two adjacent numerals + measure word + noun|Adjacent numbers can give an approximate amount. Do not put 和 between them. 一两 is used for “one or two”.|我想买两三本书。|I want to buy two or three books.|骑车七八分钟就到。|It is only seven or eight minutes away by bicycle.
11|把 + 名词 + 动词|do something to a known object|Subject + 把 + known object + verb + result or completion|把 puts a known object before the verb to emphasize what happens to it. Usually add a result, direction, location, or 了 rather than using a bare verb.|请把门关了。|Please close the door.|我把这本书看完了。|I have finished reading this book.
11|左右|approximately; about|Quantity + 左右|左右 follows a number or time expression to mean “about”. Do not add 大概 or an adjacent-number approximation unnecessarily.|他三点左右来。|He will come at about three.|这件衣服一百元左右。|This piece of clothing costs about one hundred yuan.
12|才 / 就|not until / as early as; only took|Time or duration + 才/就 + verb|才 often marks a later or slower result than expected. 就 often marks an earlier or faster one. The speaker's expectation determines the contrast.|他九点才起床。|He did not get up until nine.|我五点就到学校了。|I arrived at school as early as five.
12|把……放在/送到/给……|put … in / take … to / give … to|Subject + 把 + object + verb + 在/到/给 + destination or person|Use this 把 structure when an action changes the object's location or transfers it to someone. The destination follows the verb.|请把书放在桌子上。|Please put the book on the desk.|我把礼物送给妈妈了。|I gave the gift to my mother.
13|上/下/进/出/回/过 + 来/去|compound direction toward or away|Verb + direction + 来/去|The first directional word describes the path; 来 or 去 relates the movement to the speaker. A place object goes before 来 or 去.|他走进教室来了。|He walked into the classroom toward us.|请把书拿过来。|Please bring the book over here.
13|一边……一边……|do … while doing …|一边 + action 1 + 一边 + action 2|Use 一边 before two actions happening at the same time. Both actions are normally carried out by the same subject.|我一边吃饭一边看电视。|I eat while watching television.|她一边走一边说。|She talks while walking.
14|把……动词 + 结果/方向|cause a result or movement in an object|Subject + 把 + object + verb + result / direction|The complement states the resulting condition or movement of the known object. Keep the object before the verb and its result.|请把房间打扫干净。|Please clean the room thoroughly.|他把水果拿进来了。|He brought the fruit inside.
14|先……再/又……然后……|first …, next …, then …|先 + action 1 + 再/又 + action 2 + 然后 + action 3|These words place actions in order. 再 usually introduces the next planned action; 又 can link another completed action. 然后 introduces what follows.|先洗手，再吃饭。|Wash your hands first, then eat.|我先买了水果，然后回家。|I bought fruit first and then went home.
15|除了……以外，还/也/都……|besides … also … / except … all …|除了 + item + 以外 + 还/也/都 + statement|With 还 or 也, add another item to the first one. With 都, exclude the item and describe everyone or everything else. 以外 can be omitted.|除了茶，我还喜欢咖啡。|Besides tea, I also like coffee.|除了他，大家都来了。|Everyone came except him.
15|谁/什么……都……|anyone / anything; an unspecified person or thing|Question pronoun in a statement|Question pronouns can refer to an unspecified person or thing instead of asking a question. Their meaning depends on the surrounding clause.|我想买点儿什么。|I would like to buy something.|有什么不明白的，可以问我。|If there is anything you do not understand, you can ask me.
15|极了|extremely …|Adjective + 极了|极了 follows an adjective to express a very high degree. Keep 了 in this fixed degree expression.|今天热极了。|It is extremely hot today.|这个故事有意思极了。|This story is extremely interesting.
16|如果……就……|if …, then …|如果 + condition + 的话 + subject + 就 + result|如果 introduces a possible condition and 就 introduces its result. 的话 is optional. Put the second subject before 就.|如果下雨，我就不去了。|If it rains, I will not go.|如果你有时间，就来我家吧。|If you have time, come to my home.
16|得 + 动作/结果|so … that …|Adjective / verb + 得 + description or action|得 can introduce a longer phrase describing the degree or resulting state. Read this grammatical 得 as de, not dé or děi.|我累得不想说话。|I am so tired that I do not want to talk.|她高兴得笑了起来。|She was so happy that she started laughing.
16|形容词重叠|emphasize a descriptive quality|One-syllable adjective AA + 的|Repeating a one-syllable adjective makes the description vivid, often with a warm or subjective tone. Add 的 after the repeated adjective.|孩子的脸红红的。|The child's face is rosy red.|她的头发长长的。|Her hair is long.
17|动词重叠|do something briefly or give it a try|Two-syllable verb ABAB|Repeating a two-syllable verb suggests a short, relaxed, or tentative action. Do not add another 一 between the two copies of the whole verb.|我们休息休息吧。|Let us take a short rest.|你介绍我们认识认识吧。|Introduce us so we can get to know each other.
17|谁/什么/哪儿……都……|everyone / everything / everywhere|Question word + 都/也 + predicate|A question word followed by 都 or 也 can cover every member of a group. The negative form can mean “no one” or “nothing”, depending on the words used.|谁都喜欢她。|Everyone likes her.|这件事我什么都不知道。|I know nothing about this matter.
18|只要……就……|as long as …, then …|只要 + sufficient condition + 就 + result|只要 gives a sufficient condition: when it is met, the result follows. If the result clause has a new subject, place it before 就.|只要你来，我就高兴。|As long as you come, I will be happy.|只要认真学习，就会进步。|As long as you study seriously, you will improve.
18|关于|about; regarding|关于 + topic + statement / 的 + noun|关于 introduces the topic being discussed. It can also modify a noun, with 的 linking the topic to that noun.|我想借一本关于中国的书。|I want to borrow a book about China.|关于这件事，我们还没决定。|We have not decided about this matter yet.
19|动词 + 出来|produce something; work something out|Verb + 出来|出来 can show that an action creates or brings out a new result, rather than physical movement out of a place.|我想出来一个好办法。|I thought of a good solution.|你能把这个字写出来吗？|Can you write this character out?
19|看/听 + 出来|recognize or identify by looking/listening|看/听 + 出来|出来 after a perception verb can mean recognizing something previously unclear. Use 不出来 for an inability to identify it.|我听出来是你的声音。|I recognized your voice.|我看不出来他多大。|I cannot tell how old he is.
19|动词/形容词 + 下来|become calmer, slower, or settled|Verb / adjective + 下来|下来 can describe a change from movement to rest or from a stronger state to a weaker one.|孩子安静下来了。|The child has quietened down.|请让车慢下来。|Please slow the car down.
19|想/记 + 起来|remember; recall|想/记 + 起来|起来 can mark successful recollection. 想不起来 means being unable to remember something.|我想起来你的名字了。|I remember your name now.|我记不起来那是什么时候了。|I cannot remember when that was.
19|使 / 叫 / 让|make; cause; let|Cause + 使/叫/让 + person or thing + result|These verbs can introduce the person or thing affected by a cause. 让 and 叫 are common in conversation; 使 is more formal. Context determines whether 让 means permission or causation.|这个消息让我很高兴。|This news made me very happy.|运动使身体更健康。|Exercise makes the body healthier.
20|被|be affected by an action (passive)|Affected object + 被 + agent + verb + result|被 introduces a passive event. The affected thing comes first. The agent can be omitted, and the verb normally has a result, direction, or other detail.|我的书被他拿走了。|My book was taken away by him.|衣服被洗干净了。|The clothes have been washed clean.
20|只有……才……|only if …, then …|只有 + necessary condition + 才 + result|只有 gives a necessary condition: the result can occur only when it is met. 才 follows the subject of the result clause.|只有认真学习，才能学好汉语。|Only by studying seriously can you learn Chinese well.|只有你来，我才去。|I will go only if you come.
